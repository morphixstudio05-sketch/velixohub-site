Chintu aur Bolne Wala Tota

Ek subah Chintu ke aangan mein ek hara tota aa gaya. Uski chonch lal thi aur aankhon mein shararat. Chintu ne use kela khilaya aur naam rakh diya "Mithu".

Mithu bahut tez nikla. Wo jo bhi sunta, wahi dohra deta tha. Chintu ne socha, "Waah! Ab mere paas bolne wala dost hai."

Par musibat tab shuru hui jab Mithu mela mein pahuncha. Sarpanch ji bhashan de rahe the, tabhi upar ped se awaaz aayi, "Main roz raat ko meetha halwa chhup kar khata hoon! Hi hi hi!"

Sab log hans pade. Sarpanch ji ka chehra tamatar jaisa laal ho gaya. Unhone ghoor kar Chintu ko dekha, "Ye tera tota hai?"

Chintu ne jaldi se socha. Usne jeb se ek kela nikala aur dhire se bola, "Mithu, ye kela le. Ab bolo, 'Sarpanch ji bahut achhe hain!'"

Mithu ne kela khaya aur zor se bola, "Sarpanch ji bahut achhe hain! Sarpanch ji bahut achhe hain!" Poora mela taliyon se goonj utha. Sarpanch ji bhi muskura diye. Unhone kaha, "Chalo, aaj se halwa sabke saath baantunga."

Us raat Chintu ne Mithu ko pyar se samjhaya, "Dost, kisi ka raaz sabke saamne nahi bolte. Aur sunte hi dohra dena bhi achhi baat nahi."

Mithu ne sar hilaya aur dheere se bola, "Achhi baat nahi... achhi baat nahi!" Chintu hans pada. Agli subah usne Mithu ko ped par khula chhod diya, par tota roz shaam ko wapas aata raha, kela khane aur Chintu se baatein karne.

Seekh: Kisi ka raaz sambhal kar rakho aur muskurahat se mushkil ko aasaan bana lo.
