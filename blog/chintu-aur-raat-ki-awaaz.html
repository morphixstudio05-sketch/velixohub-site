Chintu aur Raat ki Awaaz

Chintu ke gaon mein ek purana kuan tha, jo ab sookh chuka tha. Kuchh dino se raat ko wahan se ajeeb si awaaz aa rahi thi: "Oon... oon... oon..."

Gaon ke bachche darr gaye. Kisi ne kaha, "Wahan bhoot hai!" Kisi ne kaha, "Mere dada ji bhi yahi kehte the!" Shaam hote hi sab apne gharon mein ghus jate.

Chintu ko darr to lagta tha, par usse zyada jaanne ki khujli thi. Usne apni dadi se kaha, "Dadi, bhoot ho ya na ho, pehle dekhna to padega." Dadi ne use ek lalten aur ek lambi rassi di, aur bola, "Door se dekhna, kuen ke andar mat jana."

Raat ko Chintu dabe paon kuen ke paas pahuncha. Chaand badal mein chhupa tha. Hawa sarsara rahi thi. Phir wahi awaaz aayi: "Oon... oon..."

Chintu ka dil dhak-dhak karne laga. Usne lalten uthayi aur kuen ke andar jhaanka. Neeche kuchh chamak raha tha. Do chhoti-chhoti ankhein!

Chintu peeche hata, phir himmat jutayi aur dobara dekha. Wo ek chhota sa kutte ka bachcha tha, jo andar gir gaya tha aur bhookh se ro raha tha.

Chintu hans pada. "Are, tu to bhoot nahi, mera dost hai!" Usne rassi ek mazboot ped se bandhi, bartan mein roti rakhkar neeche bheji, aur dheere-dheere bachche ko upar kheench liya.

Subah poore gaon ne dekha ki Chintu ke saath ek chhota kutta poonchh hilata ghoom raha hai. Sab hairan the. Chintu ne kaha, "Jo awaaz samajh nahi aati, usse bhaagna nahi, pehle dekhna chahiye."

Us din se kutte ka naam "Bhootu" pad gaya, aur wo poore gaon ka pyara dost ban gaya. Raat ko ab kuen se koi awaaz nahi aati, bas Bhootu ki poonchh hilti dikhti hai.

Seekh: Darr ke peeche ka sach jaanne ki koshish karo, aur jisko madad chahiye use madad do.
